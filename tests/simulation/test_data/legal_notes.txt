CASE NOTE 005
Date: January 21, 2025
Case: Voss v. Kellerman & Drape
File No.: CB-2025-002
Attorney: Imani Foster
Note Type: Research Memo — Whistleblower Protections

Illinois Whistleblower Act (740 ILCS 174) protections confirmed applicable:
- Voss reported suspected illegal activity (billing fraud) to internal compliance
- Termination followed 65 days after complaint — temporal proximity supports retaliation inference
- "Performance issues" cited in termination letter undercut by Voss's performance reviews — rated "Exceeds Expectations" in both 2022 and 2023 reviews

Comparable verdict research: Solis v. Meridian Partners (N.D. Ill. 2022) — $340,000 jury award for wrongful termination with similar whistleblower facts. Damages included back pay, front pay, and emotional distress.

Risk assessment: Strong facts for Voss. Weakness is the internal complaint only went to compliance (not external regulator) — some courts require external reporting for full IWA protection. However, Illinois courts have broadly construed "report" to include internal compliance under IWA.

Recommend: Proceed with demand letter, then litigation if no acceptable settlement.

Imani Foster, Associate
