Proposal attached. Summary:

Message queue: Kafka as suggested — agreed it's the right call. I looked at Pulsar as an alternative but the operational complexity isn't worth it for our scale. Kafka it is.

Analytics store: I'm pushing back on ClickHouse. Our query patterns are more relational than analytical. I want to use TimescaleDB instead — it's Postgres-based so our team knows the query language, and the time-series compression is excellent for event data.

Ingestion layer: I'm giving Tobias the Kafka consumer workers. He's been asking for more ownership and this is well-scoped.

Deployment: everything containerized with Docker Compose for local dev, Kubernetes in prod. Leo will need to be looped in on the K8s side.

Estimated timeline: 8 weeks to production-ready. I can do 6 if we drop the backfill feature to v2.

One risk I want to flag: Tobias has never worked with Kafka before. I'm building in two weeks of ramp-up time for him.
